## 13. FAQ (layout A)

Questions
Good questions, plain answers.
FAQ answers below are approved. Use FAQPage schema.

Can't find yours? Ask us on WhatsApp and we'll reply within working hours.
Ask on WhatsApp
How much does a consultation cost?
₹500, including an examination by the dentist. X-rays, if needed, are charged separately and explained first.
Which clinic should I visit?
Whichever is closer. Both dentists consult at both clinics on set days, so call to check who's in.
Are you open on weekends?
Yes, Saturday and Sunday. We're closed only on Wednesdays.
Do you treat children?
Yes. Dr Parmod Gulia is a specialist pediatric dentist (MDS, PGIMER Chandigarh), and he sees babies, children, teens and children with special needs.
Is a root canal painful?
It's done under local anaesthesia, and most patients say it feels like a long filling. We'll tell you what to expect before we start.
How long does an implant take?
Usually 3 to 6 months from placing the implant to fixing the final tooth, because the bone needs time to heal around it. Dr Ruchi will give you a timeline at your consultation.
Can I get a second opinion?
Of course. Bring your X-rays and the treatment plan you were given.
Do you accept insurance?
Call us to check your plan. We'll give you an itemised bill for reimbursement.
What should I do in a dental emergency?
Call the nearest clinic. During working hours we try to see urgent cases the same day.
