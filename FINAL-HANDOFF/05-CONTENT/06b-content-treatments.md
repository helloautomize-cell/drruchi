## 1. Dental Implants  `/dental-implants`
**Meta:** Dental Implants in Gurgaon | AIIMS Prosthodontist | Dental Krafts. *Replace missing teeth with implants planned by Dr Ruchi Goel (MDS, AIIMS). Typical cost ₹30,000–₹55,000. Real results, written estimate.*
**Who treats:** Dr Ruchi Goel · **Cost chip:** ₹30,000 – ₹55,000 per tooth

**H1:** Dental implants in Gurgaon
**Sub:** A fixed tooth that looks, feels and chews like your own, planned by an AIIMS-trained prosthodontist.

**Is this for you?**
- You've lost one or more teeth to decay, gum disease or an accident
- A removable denture or bridge feels loose or uncomfortable
- You want to stop neighbouring teeth from shifting into the gap
- You'd like to bite into an apple again without thinking about it

**How an implant works:** A small titanium post is placed in the jawbone where the root used to be. Over 3–6 months the bone bonds to it. A connector (abutment) and a custom crown then sit on top, so you get a tooth that stays put and doesn't need neighbouring teeth to be cut down, as a bridge does.

**Your options**
- **Single implant:** one missing tooth, one implant, one crown.
- **Implant bridge:** several missing teeth in a row, held by two or more implants.
- **All-on-4 / All-on-6:** a full set of fixed teeth on four to six implants. See the All-on-4 page.
- **Implant-retained denture:** your denture clicks onto two to four implants, so it no longer slips.
- **With bone grafting:** if bone has shrunk after a long gap, it can often be rebuilt first.

**Step by step**
1. **Consultation & scan.** Dr Ruchi examines you and checks bone height and width on an X-ray or 3D scan.
2. **Plan & estimate.** You see where the implant will go, the timeline and the cost, in writing.
3. **Placement.** A short procedure under local anaesthesia, usually about an hour for one implant.
4. **Healing (3–6 months).** You may wear a temporary tooth. The bone bonds to the implant.
5. **Final crown.** We take an impression and fit a zirconia or ceramic crown matched to your teeth.
6. **Reviews.** Check-ups and cleaning keep the implant healthy for the long term.

**Cost**
| Item | Typical range |
|---|---|
| Consultation & X-rays | ₹500 – ₹2,000 |
| Implant placement | ₹20,000 – ₹40,000 |
| Abutment & crown | ₹10,000 – ₹20,000 |
| **Implant + crown, typical total** | **₹30,000 – ₹55,000** |
| Bone graft, sinus lift (if needed) | quoted after scan |

**Aftercare:** Soft food and no hard chewing on that side for the first few days. Brush and floss around the implant like a natural tooth. Don't smoke, since it raises the risk of failure. Come in for a check-up every 6 months.

**FAQs**
1. *Does getting an implant hurt?* It's done under local anaesthesia. Most patients say it was easier than an extraction. Mild soreness for 2–3 days is normal, and painkillers handle it.
2. *How long do implants last?* With good care and regular check-ups, many last 15–20 years or more. The crown on top may need replacing sooner.
3. *Am I too old for an implant?* Age isn't the limit. Bone health and general health are. Many of our implant patients are in their 60s and 70s.
4. *I have diabetes. Can I get an implant?* Usually yes, if it's well controlled. We'll check with you and your physician.
5. *Why is there such a range in cost?* It depends on the implant system, the crown material, and whether you need bone grafting. Your estimate will itemise each.

## 5. Root Canal Treatment  `/root-canal-treatment`
**Meta:** Root Canal Treatment in Gurgaon | ₹6,000–₹12,000 | Dental Krafts. *Save your tooth and stop the pain. Root canal under local anaesthesia, often in one visit. AIIMS & PGIMER-trained dentists.*
**Who treats:** Dr Parmod Gulia · **Cost chip:** ₹6,000 – ₹12,000 per tooth
**Urgent CTA above the fold:** Call now: Sector 53 · Sector 57

**H1:** Root canal treatment in Gurgaon
**Sub:** Stop the pain and save your own tooth. Done under local anaesthesia, often in a single visit.

**Is this for you?**
- A throbbing toothache, especially at night
- Pain with hot or cold that lingers after the drink is gone
- Swelling or a pimple on the gum
- A tooth that has darkened after an injury
- A deep cavity or a cracked tooth

**How it works:** Inside every tooth is soft tissue (the pulp). When decay or a crack lets bacteria in, the pulp gets infected and hurts. A root canal removes the infected pulp, cleans and shapes the canals, and seals them, so the tooth stays and the pain goes.

**Step by step**
1. X-ray and diagnosis
2. Local anaesthesia, so the tooth is numb
3. Infected pulp removed, canals cleaned and shaped
4. Canals filled and sealed
5. A crown is usually advised on back teeth to stop the tooth cracking

**Cost**
| Item | Typical range |
|---|---|
| Root canal, one tooth | ₹6,000 – ₹12,000 |
| Re-treatment of an old root canal | quoted after X-ray ⚠ |
| Crown after root canal | ₹12,000 – ₹20,000 |

**Aftercare:** Avoid chewing on the tooth until the numbness wears off and until the crown is fitted. Mild tenderness for 2–3 days is normal. Call us if swelling increases.

**FAQs**
1. *Is a root canal painful?* The treatment itself is done under local anaesthesia. Most patients say it felt like a long filling. It's the infection that hurts, and the root canal stops it.
2. *Can it be done in one visit?* Often, yes. Badly infected teeth may need two.
3. *Why do I need a crown afterwards?* A root-treated back tooth is more brittle. A crown protects it from cracking.
4. *Wouldn't it be cheaper to remove the tooth?* Today, yes. But replacing it later with an implant or bridge costs more. Keeping your own tooth is usually the better long-term choice.
