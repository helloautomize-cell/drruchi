Frequently Asked Questions
               What constitutes a dental emergency?
Severe toothaches, knocked-out teeth, broken or cracked teeth, abscesses, and injuries to the mouth or jaw are common dental emergencies.
               What should I do in case of a dental emergency?
Manage pain with over-the-counter medications, rinse your mouth with warm water, and preserve knocked-out teeth in milk or saliva until you can see a dentist.
               Do you offer emergency dental appointments at Dental Krafts?
Dental Krafts provides same-day or next-day emergency appointments to address urgent dental issues promptly.
               What emergency dental treatments do you provide at Dental Krafts?
We offer tooth extractions, dental fillings, root canal therapy, oral infection treatment, and dental trauma repairs.
               How can I prevent dental emergencies in the future?
Maintain good oral hygiene, wear protective mouthguards during sports, avoid chewing complex objects, and schedule regular dental check-ups to catch issues early.
               How to prevent a dental emergency?
To prevent dental emergencies:
Be mindful of your oral health, avoid foods that exert excessive pressure on your teeth, and reduce the consumption of sugary fizzy drinks.
If you engage in sports like cricket or boxing, always wear a mouthguard to prevent tooth breakage and other injuries.
Maintain good oral hygiene by brushing and flossing daily. For personalized care and additional tips, please consult with our doctors at Dental Krafts during your visit.
               What should I do if I experience sudden tooth pain?
If you're facing sudden toothache:
Avoid putting pressure on your teeth by refraining from eating or drinking.
Rinse your mouth with lukewarm water and apply a cold compress for pain relief.
