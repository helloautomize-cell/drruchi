What Are Dental Implants?
Dental implants
 are artificial tooth roots made from titanium that are surgically inserted into the jawbone. They provide a strong foundation for 
dental crowns
 or bridges, designed to match your natural teeth. They can also work with 
dentures
 for full-mouth restoration.
Benefits of Dental Implants
Permanent solution for missing teeth
Natural look and feel, often paired with 
dental veneers
Improved speech and chewing
Prevention of jawbone loss
Boost in confidence and smile aesthetics, similar to 
smile makeover
