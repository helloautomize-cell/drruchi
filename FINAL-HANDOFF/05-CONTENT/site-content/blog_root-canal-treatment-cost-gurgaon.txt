Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)
1. What is a Root Canal Treatment (RCT)?
A root canal is a dental procedure to remove infected pulp from inside the tooth, clean and disinfect it, and seal it to prevent future infections. Sometimes a 
dental filling
 may also be used as part of the process.
2. Is a root canal painful?
No, modern root canal treatments at Dental Krafts are painless. We use advanced anesthesia techniques to ensure patient comfort.
3. How long does a root canal procedure take?
A standard root canal usually takes 60–90 minutes and may require 1–3 sittings depending on the severity of the infection. Single-sitting RCT is also available.
4. How much does a root canal cost in Gurgaon?
The cost varies based on the tooth type and technology used. It ranges from ₹6,000 to ₹12,000 for a single tooth. Additional costs may apply for dental crowns.
5. Why do I need a dental crown after a root canal?
A crown protects and strengthens the treated tooth, preventing fractures or further decay. Ceramic crowns at Dental Krafts cost between ₹12,000 and ₹20,000.
6. How do I know if I need a root canal?
Common signs include severe tooth pain, sensitivity to hot/cold, gum swelling, and darkening of the tooth. A 
consultation with our dentist
 can confirm the need for an RCT.
7. Is a root canal better than tooth extraction?
Yes, a root canal preserves your natural tooth, while an extraction may require additional treatments like dental implants or crowns/bridges to restore function and aesthetics.
8. Do you offer EMI or flexible payment options?
Yes! We provide affordable pricing and EMI options to make the treatment accessible for everyone.
