Our Popular Dental Treatments
Cosmetic Dentistry
Cosmetic dentistry can improve the colour, shape, alignment and overall appearance of your smile. Depending on your concern, our dentists may recommend teeth whitening, dental veneers, bonding, crowns or a complete smile makeover.
Explore
Dental Crowns
Dental crowns protect and restore teeth weakened by decay, fractures or root canal treatment. We evaluate the affected tooth and recommend a suitable crown material based on strength, appearance, location and long-term care needs.
Explore
Dental Fillings
Dental fillings repair teeth affected by cavities, minor fractures or wear. Our dentists remove the damaged portion of the tooth and select a filling material based on the tooth’s condition, location and aesthetic requirements.
Explore
Dental Implants
Dental implants provide a fixed replacement option for one or more missing teeth. After evaluating your bone health, gums and medical history, our implant dentist explains the procedure, restoration options, expected timeline and aftercare.
Explore
Dental Veneers
Dental veneers are thin restorations placed over the front surface of selected teeth. They may help improve concerns such as tooth discolouration, small gaps, uneven shape or minor surface damage after a proper dental assessment.
Explore
Dentures
Dentures replace multiple missing teeth and help support everyday functions such as speaking and chewing. Dental Krafts provides complete and partial denture options planned according to oral condition, comfort, fit and maintenance needs.
Explore
Teeth Cleaning
Professional teeth cleaning removes plaque and hardened deposits that regular brushing may not fully remove. Routine cleaning can support gum health, fresher breath and better oral hygiene when recommended after a dental examination.
Explore
Root Canal Treatment
Root canal treatment may be recommended when decay, injury or infection reaches the inner part of a tooth. The procedure removes infected tissue, cleans the canals and helps preserve the natural tooth whenever clinically possible.
Explore
Gum disease
Gum disease treatment depends on the severity of inflammation, bleeding, gum recession or bone loss. Our dentists assess your gum health and may recommend professional cleaning, deep cleaning or further periodontal care.
Explore
Braces Treatment
Braces gradually correct crooked, crowded or improperly aligned teeth. After examining your bite and alignment, our dentist explains suitable orthodontic options, expected treatment duration and the care required during treatment.
Explore
Invisalign Treatment
Invisalign uses a series of removable clear aligners to gradually improve suitable alignment concerns. Treatment planning begins with a dental assessment to determine whether clear aligners are appropriate for your teeth and bite.
Explore
Paediatric Dentist
Our paediatric dental care includes routine check-ups, cavity management, preventive treatments and guidance on children’s oral hygiene. The team uses an age-appropriate approach to help children feel comfortable during dental visits.
