Frequently Asked Questions
                     Can cosmetic dentistry improve the health of my smile?
Yes. While cosmetic dentistry is mainly intended to improve the visual aspects of your smile, many cosmetic procedures offer restorative improvement and can boost the health of your smile. For example, correcting misalignment removes hiding places of harmful bacteria, reducing the risk of tooth decay and gum disease. Other treatments, such as Botox, can ease muscle tension and reduce the symptoms of bruxism and TMJ disorder.
                     What are the costs for these procedures?
The foremost question for people looking for cosmetic dentistry procedures would be, "How much would it cost me?". Rates depend on the procedure you want and its complexity, and they also vary from dentist to dentist. Some are expensive, and some are suited for those with a limited budget. Most of the procedures are costly, but people are often judged by their first impression, so it is worth it.
                     What common dental problems are suitable for cosmetic dentistry?
People may want to visit a dentist for cosmetic dental problems for multiple reasons. You may visit the dentist for cosmetic purposes if you have any of the following concerns:
Stained or discoloured teeth:
  Stained or discoloured teeth can make you self-conscious about your smile. With teeth whitening, dental bonding, or veneers, your teeth can have an even, natural-looking colour across your smile.
Gaps in between teeth: 
Big or small gaps in your teeth can cause you to hide your smile. These can often be filled in almost instantly with dental bonding or veneers.
Uneven teeth:  
If your teeth are not perfectly straight, they can draw attention to themselves. Uneven teeth are often treated with dental bonding or veneers to create the appearance of straighter teeth.
Worn-down teeth
: Teeth that are cracked or chipped can become more damaged over time, thus becoming less functional and susceptible to decay. Missing enamel can be replaced with dental bonding, fillings, crowns, and veneers.
Excess gum tissue:
  Excess gum tissue may make your teeth appear short. This can be fixed through a quick and painless procedure of tissue laser treatment.
                     Why should I choose cosmetic dentistry over at-home treatments?
Nothing can replace professional help, so having your teeth checked and corrected by a dentist is always worth it. They can examine your teeth and determine whether there are concerns and what requires immediate action other than common teeth problems. Usually, the cost of professional teeth whitening is more than at-home treatment, but using a home kit can increase the risks of sensitivity to your gums or dentin. Moreover, dentists do this regularly, and they understand the procedure that provides a satisfying or quick result.
                     Will my insurance cover my treatment?
Whether or not your insurance covers these procedures is a function of what treatment you're getting and what your individual company's policy is. Though specific insurance plans cover some cosmetic procedures, not all will be. 
 Insurance plans usually focus on procedures that are deemed medically necessary. Since these procedures are cosmetic, they confer no medical benefit besides the improved teeth look, so they may not be covered.
                     What is a smile makeover?
A smile makeover combines various cosmetic dental treatments to enhance the appearance of your smile, fixing issues like discoloration, misalignment, gaps, or chips.
                     What procedures are involved in Smile Designing?
Treatments may include teeth whitening, veneers, dental bonding, orthodontics, gum contouring, and dental implants tailored to your needs.
                     How long does this cosmetic procedure take?
The duration depends on the complexity, ranging from a few appointments to several months.
                     Is Smile makeover for everyone?
A comprehensive dental evaluation determines suitability based on oral health, dental history, and treatment goals.
                     What are the benefits of Smile Designing?
Enjoy improved aesthetics, increased confidence, better oral health, and enhanced dental function, leading to a youthful appearance and better quality of life.
