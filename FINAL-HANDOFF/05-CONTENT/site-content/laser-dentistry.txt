What is Laser Dentistry?
Laser dentistry is an advanced dental technique that uses focused light energy to treat soft tissues in the mouth. Instead of traditional tools like blades or drills, lasers allow 
dentists
 to perform procedures with greater precision and control.
This approach minimizes damage to surrounding tissues, reduces discomfort, and speeds up healing - making it ideal for both routine and complex dental treatments.
How Laser Dentistry Works
Laser dentistry works by sending a controlled light beam to the targeted area inside the mouth. This light energy helps remove infected tissue, clean bacteria, or reshape the gums without affecting healthy areas. The process is very precise, which reduces unnecessary damage and improves overall results. At Dental Krafts, each procedure is carefully planned so the treatment stays safe and accurate. Since lasers also help in disinfecting the area, the chances of infection are lower. This makes the entire process smoother, cleaner, and more comfortable compared to traditional dental methods used in Gurgaon.

FAQ’s About Laser Dentistry in Gurgaon
               Is laser dentistry painful?
Laser dentistry is usually painless and much more comfortable than traditional methods. In most cases, patients do not feel much discomfort, and anesthesia is often not required or used in very minimal amounts.
               Is laser dental treatment safe?
Yes, laser dental treatment is completely safe when performed by trained professionals. It is a modern and advanced method that ensures precise treatment with reduced risk of infection and better healing.
               How long does recovery take after laser treatment?
Recovery after laser dental treatment is generally faster compared to traditional procedures. Most patients experience minimal discomfort, less swelling, and can return to normal activities within a short time.
               Can laser dentistry replace traditional treatments?
In many cases, Laser Dentistry in Gurgaon can replace traditional dental methods, especially for soft tissue treatments. However, it depends on the type and severity of the dental condition being treated.
               Is laser treatment suitable for children?
Yes, laser dental treatment is safe and suitable for children. It is gentle, less painful, and does not involve loud tools, making it a comfortable and stress-free option for young patients.
