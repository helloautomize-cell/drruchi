Dr Parmod Gulia
Pediatric and Preventive Dentistry
BDS, MDS (PGIMER Chandigarh), Pediatric and Preventive Dentistry; Silver Medalist, PGIMER Chandigarh; formerly at AIIMS New Delhi.
Dr Parmod Gulia provides dental care for children and adolescents, including preventive treatment and care for patients with special needs. He trained at PGIMER Chandigarh, where he was awarded the silver medal, and worked as a senior research fellow at AIIMS New Delhi.
He also has experience in general dentistry and root-canal treatment, and takes time to explain procedures to patients and parents.
