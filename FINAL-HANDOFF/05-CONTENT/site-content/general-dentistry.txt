Here's a brief overview of some of our primary services to help you understand when to schedule a visit to the dentist.
Porcelain Crowns:
 Dentists favoured these for their natural appearance and for restoring front teeth due to their biocompatibility. Despite being less robust than metal crowns, they are an excellent choice, especially for individuals with bruxism.
Porcelain Fused to Metal Crowns:
 Referred to as PFM, these crowns blend durability with the advantages of both metal and porcelain. While cost-effective, there may be better choices for individuals with a teeth-clenching habit.
All Ceramic Crowns:
 These are ideal for individuals allergic to metals. Despite the drawback of potential wear on opposing teeth, dentists often prefer this material for front teeth due to its natural appearance.
Gold Crowns:
These are strong  durable, well-suited for restoring natural teeth at the back, boasting a low wear rate. However, they do not mimic the appearance of natural teeth, making them more suitable for covering back molars.
Zirconia Crowns:
 Crafted from zirconium, these dental crowns exhibit longevity, strength, and biocompatibility. Notably, these crowns offer the advantage of easy shaping without waiting for confirmation from the dental laboratory.
