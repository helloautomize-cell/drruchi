Do and don't

The rules that keep it calm.

Do
  • Forest for every primary action
  • One italic rose word per headline
  • Photos as the decoration
  • Show each rating with source, count and date
  • Label which phone belongs to which clinic
  • Keep the watermark on results
  • Indian English and ₹1,50,000 grouping
Don't
  • Orange, plum or blue anywhere
  • Gradients, blobs, circles or wave dividers
  • White text on clay
  • 3D emoji or clip-art icons
  • "Painless guaranteed", "cheapest", "gold medalist"
  • A clinic founding year, until confirmed
  • AI people next to results
