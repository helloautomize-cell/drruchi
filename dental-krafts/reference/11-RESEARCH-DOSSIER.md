## 10. Compliance guardrails (India)
- The Dental Council of India's code of ethics restricts dentists' self-promotion. Avoid unqualified superlatives in on-page headings ("Best dentist in Gurgaon"). Keep "best" for SEO titles and meta only if the client accepts the risk. **⚠ client decision.**
- Before/after photos: get written patient consent on file. Crop to the mouth where possible and keep the watermark.
- Never promise outcomes ("painless guaranteed"). Use "comfort-focused", "local anaesthesia", "we explain every step".
- Show registration numbers on doctor pages. That builds trust and helps with compliance.
