### FAQ (home)
1. **How much does a consultation cost?** ₹500, including an examination by the dentist. X-rays, if needed, are charged separately and explained first.
2. **Which clinic should I visit?** Whichever is closer. Both dentists consult at both clinics on set days, so call to check who's in.
3. **Are you open on weekends?** Yes, Saturday and Sunday. We're closed only on Wednesdays.
4. **Do you treat children?** Yes. Dr Parmod Gulia is a specialist pediatric dentist (MDS, PGIMER Chandigarh), and he sees babies, children, teens and children with special needs.
5. **Is a root canal painful?** It's done under local anaesthesia, and most patients say it feels like a long filling. We'll tell you what to expect before we start.
6. **How long does an implant take?** Usually 3 to 6 months from placing the implant to fixing the final tooth, because the bone needs time to heal around it. Dr Ruchi will give you a timeline at your consultation.
7. **Can I get a second opinion?** Of course. Bring your X-rays and the treatment plan you were given.
8. **Do you accept insurance?** ⚠ Many plans are accepted. Call us to check yours, and we'll give you an itemised bill for reimbursement.
9. **What should I do in a dental emergency?** Call the nearest clinic. During working hours we try to see urgent cases the same day.

### FAQ
1. **When should my child first see a dentist?** By their first birthday, or within six months of the first tooth. Early visits are short, friendly and mostly about prevention.
2. **Why see a pediatric dentist instead of a general dentist?** Children's teeth, behaviour and growth are different. A pediatric dentist has three more years of training in exactly that.
3. **Do milk teeth really need fillings?** Yes, usually. Milk teeth hold space for adult teeth, and an untreated cavity can cause pain, infection and crowding later.
4. **What is treatment under general anaesthesia?** For very young or anxious children, or children with special needs, who need a lot of treatment, it's done in a hospital operating theatre with an anaesthetist, all in one session. We'll explain the costs separately (hospital charges are set by the hospital).
