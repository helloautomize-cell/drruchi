Dental Crowns
Dental crowns are caps that cover damaged or weakened teeth, restoring their shape, strength, and appearance. They protect the natural tooth underneath, preventing further issues like decay or injury, while blending in with your other teeth for a natural look.

What are the Types of Dental Crowns?
Types of dental Crowns depend on the materials used, which are as follows:
Porcelain Crowns:
 Dentists favoured these for their natural appearance and for restoring front teeth due to their biocompatibility. Despite being less robust than metal crowns, they are an excellent choice, especially for individuals with bruxism.
Porcelain Fused to Metal Crowns:
 Referred to as PFM, these crowns blend durability with the advantages of both metal and porcelain. While cost-effective, there may be better choices for individuals with a teeth-clenching habit.
All Ceramic Crowns:
 These are ideal for individuals allergic to metals. Despite the drawback of potential wear on opposing teeth, dentists often prefer this material for front teeth due to its natural appearance.
Gold Crowns:
 These are strong  durable, well-suited for restoring natural teeth at the back, boasting a low wear rate. However, they do not mimic the appearance of natural teeth, making them more suitable for covering back molars.
Zirconia Crowns:
 Crafted from zirconium, these dental crowns exhibit longevity, strength, and biocompatibility. Notably, these crowns offer the advantage of easy shaping without waiting for confirmation from the dental laboratory.
The crowns' choice depends on the damage's extent and the procedure's purpose.

Step-by-Step Process of Placing a Permanent or Temporary Dental Crown
Dental Krafts uses a systematic approach to offer customized 
dental crown services in Gurgaon
 as per requirement.
Preliminary Inspection:
 Experts assess your mouth, conduct X-ray scans, and check for decay, misalignment, infection, or cavities. Root canal treatment may be necessary.
Initial Preparation of the Tooth:
 After discussing crown options, the dentist readies the decayed tooth by filing its top and side surfaces, creating space for crown insertion. Local anesthesia may be used. The extent of material removal depends on the crown material; metal crowns need less space, so filing is adjusted accordingly.
Creating Impression of the Primary Tooth:
 An impression, made with putty or a digital scanner, is sent to the dental lab for manufacturing the crown, a process taking a minimum of 3 weeks. It is crucial to cover the entire tooth and safeguard the natural enamel. In the interim, a temporary dental crown is provided.
Fitment of Crown on the Dental Implant or Tooth:
 Temporary crowns safeguard teeth until permanent ones are available but must be adequately removed for the final step. The dentist evaluates the fit and colour of the new crown, using local anesthesia before its permanent cementing, ensuring a natural look, especially for ceramic and porcelain crowns.
What are Post-care Tips for Dental Crowns?
At Dental Krafts, our appointment sessions cover pre-procedure discussions, temporary restoration recommendations, details on crown types, cracked tooth care, assessment for permanent crown need, and selecting suitable crown types. We also provide aftercare tips for proper crown maintenance. Patients should follow professional instructions for caring for temporary and permanent crowns.
To care for temporary crowns:
Use dental floss by sliding, not lifting, between the crown and adjacent teeth.
Initially, chew using opposing teeth.
Maintain regular cleaning to remove debris around the crown.
Avoid hard foods to prevent crown loosening.
If the crown falls, contact the dentist promptly.
If loose, avoid wearing it at night.
Whether zirconia or another type, proper care is crucial for temporary caps.

Frequently Asked Questions
                     What are dental crowns, and when are they needed?
Dental crowns are like caps placed over damaged or weakened teeth to restore their shape, size, strength, and look. They're needed when teeth have big fillings, extensive decay, fractures, or after 
root canal treatment
.
                     What materials are used for dental crowns at Dental Krafts?
Dental Krafts offers different crown materials, such as porcelain, ceramic, metal, and porcelain-fused-to-metal (PFM). Each has its benefits, and our dentists will help you choose the best one.
                     How long do dental crowns last?
The lifespan of dental crowns varies based on materials, oral hygiene, and biting forces. Generally, they can last between 5 to 15 years or even more with good care.
                     How is the dental crown process at Dental Krafts?
It's usually a two-visit process. First, we prepare the tooth by removing decay and taking impressions for a custom crown. Then, we fit a temporary crown. During the second visit, we place the permanent crown.
                     Are there any alternatives to dental crowns?
Alternatives like dental fillings, inlays, onlays, or veneers might be possible depending on your tooth's condition. Our dentists will assess and suggest the best option to restore your smile and oral health.
