Frequently Asked Questions About Smile Designing & Smile Correction
1. Is Smile Designing permanent?
Smile Designing results, like 
veneers
 and crowns, can last 10–15 years with proper care.
2. Does Smile Designing hurt?
No, it's a painless process, often performed with minimally invasive techniques like dental fillings or bonding.
3. How long does Smile Designing take?
Simple cases may take 1–2 weeks, complex corrections may require 1–2 months.
4. Is DSD necessary?
DSD is highly recommended for accurate, predictable results and visualizing the final smile.
5. Is Smile Designing covered by insurance?
No, it is considered a cosmetic procedure and typically not covered by insurance.
