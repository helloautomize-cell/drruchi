FAQs About Teeth Whitening in Gurgaon
1. Is teeth whitening safe?
Yes! When performed by a professional under 
general dentistry
, it is safe and effective.
2. How long do teeth whitening results last?
With good oral hygiene, professional whitening results can last 1-3 years. Regular 
teeth cleaning
 helps maintain results longer.
3. Can I eat normally after teeth whitening?
Avoid staining foods (coffee, tea, wine, turmeric) for 24-48 hours after treatment.
4. Does teeth whitening cause sensitivity?
Some people experience mild sensitivity, which usually subsides within 24-48 hours. Consultation with a 
tooth pain specialist
 can help if sensitivity persists.
5. Does dental insurance cover teeth whitening?
No, as it is a cosmetic procedure, most insurance plans do not cover it. For alternatives, 
cosmetic dentistry
 can offer professional solutions.
