Scaling and root planing

Frequently Asked Questions
                     How often should I get my teeth cleaned by a dentist?
Professional teeth cleaning is recommended every six months. Some people with specific oral health issues may need more frequent cleanings. Regular cleanings help remove plaque and tartar, preventing tooth decay and gum disease.
                     What does a teeth cleaning procedure involve?
A dental hygienist or dentist will use special tools to remove plaque and tartar from your teeth, polish your teeth to remove surface stains, and apply fluoride to strengthen your teeth and prevent cavities.
                     Is teeth cleaning painful?
Teeth cleaning is usually not painful, though you may feel discomfort or sensitivity, especially if you have sensitive teeth or gum disease. The dental professional can use gentle techniques and numbing agents to ensure comfort.
                     Can teeth cleaning improve the appearance of my smile?
Regular cleanings can brighten your smile by removing surface stains from food, drinks, and tobacco. They also help prevent bad breath and maintain overall oral health.
                     Are there any risks or side effects associated with teeth cleaning?
Teeth cleanings
 are generally safe. Some people may experience temporary tooth sensitivity or gum irritation, especially if they have sensitive teeth or gum disease. Rarely, more severe side effects like enamel damage or gum recession can occur, but these are usually avoidable with proper technique and professional care.
