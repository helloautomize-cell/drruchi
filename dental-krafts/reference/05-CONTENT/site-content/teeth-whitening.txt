Why Do Teeth Get Discolored?
It is usually a long process involving multiple factors. Your teeth constantly wear down if exposed to acidic food and drinks. This causes thinning of the enamel. The inner part of the teeth, called dentin, also starts becoming yellow. The dentin then tries to repair itself and turns darker in the process. This dark dentin, now easily visible through the thin enamel, causes your teeth to appear yellow. Sometimes, the dentin also turns grey if it acquires a food stain. Here are the most common causes of yellow teeth:
Genetics
Excessive acidic food like citrus, berries, and vinegar
Composite fillings
Teeth grinding
Smoking or chewing tobacco
Ageing and teeth wear
Regular consumption of coffee, tea, or red wine
Fluorosis from having excessive fluoride in oral care products
A tooth disorder called Amelogenesis Imperfecta (AI)
Use of Tetracycline Antibiotics

Are you Eligible for Teeth Whitening?
The expert dentists at Dental Krafts only use safe and over-the-counter products for your teeth whitening procedure. However, the process might not suit you under certain conditions. Take a look. 
If you have sensitive teeth and gums,
Tooth whitening temporarily makes your enamel more porous, exposing your tooth's nerves and causing further sensitivity.
If you are pregnant or breastfeeding:
The hormonal changes and morning sickness during pregnancy can cause tooth erosion and leave you at higher risk of gingivitis and cavities.
If you have cavities:
An untreated cavity can become painful when subjected to whitening agents. We recommend treating the cavity before going for whitening.
If you have receding gums:
Leaving your roots exposed when you have receding gums creates more significant threats of tooth decay.

Frequently Asked Questions
               How does teeth whitening work?
Teeth whitening uses bleaching agents like hydrogen peroxide or carbamide peroxide to break up stains and discoloration on the enamel, making your teeth look brighter.
               Is teeth whitening safe?
Teeth whitening or brightening is generally safe when supervised by a dentist or using dentist-prescribed products. Overuse or improper application can cause sensitivity, gum irritation, or enamel damage. Always consult a dental professional before starting any whitening treatment.
               How long does teeth whitening last?
Whitening results can last from several months to a few years, depending on oral hygiene, diet, and lifestyle choices. Periodic touch-ups may be needed to maintain the desired whiteness.
               Can anyone undergo teeth whitening?
Teeth whitening is safe for most people but may not suit those with gum disease, cavities, or susceptible teeth. Some stains caused by medications or dental trauma may not respond well to whitening. Consult a dentist to see if whitening is right for you.
               Are there different methods of teeth whitening or brightening available?
Yes, methods include in-office treatments by dentists, take-home kits from dentists, over-the-counter products like toothpaste and strips, and natural remedies like activated charcoal or baking soda. Professional treatments usually offer the most significant and long-lasting results.
