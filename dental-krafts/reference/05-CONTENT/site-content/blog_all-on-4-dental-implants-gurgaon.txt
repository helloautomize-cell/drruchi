All-on-4 Dental Implants in Gurgaon
Date: 6-Mar-2025
 Are you looking for a permanent and cost-effective solution for multiple missing teeth? All-on-4 
dental implants
 offer a revolutionary way to restore your smile without the need for individual implants for each missing tooth. This treatment is ideal for patients with severe tooth loss and limited bone density. In this guide, we will explore the benefits, procedure, costs and key considerations for All-on-4 implants. 
What Are All-on-4 Dental Implants?
 All-on-4 is a full-arch dental implant solution that uses only four strategically placed titanium implants to support an entire set of upper or lower teeth. Unlike traditional implants, which require one post per missing tooth, the All-on-4 method maximizes existing bone structure, reducing the need for bone grafting. Many patients also combine this treatment with 
cosmetic dentistry
 for enhanced aesthetics. 
Key Features of All-on-4
Fixed full-arch restoration with only four implants
Minimally invasive surgery with faster recovery
Immediate function in many cases (teeth in a day)
Cost-effective alternative to individual implants
Suitable for patients with bone loss (often avoiding bone grafting)
Benefits of All-on-4 Dental Implants
Permanent Tooth Replacement: Provides a long-term solution that mimics natural teeth in function and appearance.
Enhanced Stability: The strategic placement of implants ensures a secure and stable fit.
Improved Oral Function: Restores chewing efficiency and improves speech clarity.
Prevents Bone Loss: Helps maintain jawbone integrity and prevents facial collapse.
Faster Recovery: Requires fewer implants, reducing healing time compared to traditional methods.
Aesthetic Improvement: Custom-designed prosthetics enhance smile aesthetics and confidence. Patients often pair this with 
teeth whitening
 or 
dental veneers
 for a complete smile makeover.

The All-on-4 Procedure: Step by Step
1. Initial Consultation & Evaluation
Comprehensive dental examination (X-rays, CT scans)
Assessment of jawbone density and overall oral health
Treatment planning based on patient needs, often part of 
budget treatments
2. Implant Placement Surgery
Local anesthesia or sedation is administered
Four implants are placed in strategic locations within the jaw
Two implants are positioned at an angle for maximum stability
Patients often ask about 
implant safety
 before undergoing this step.
3. Temporary Prosthesis Attachment (Same-day teeth option)
A temporary full-arch prosthetic is placed immediately after surgery in most cases
4. Healing & Osseointegration (3-6 months)
Implants integrate with the jawbone for a strong foundation
Dietary adjustments and follow-up visits for monitoring
Maintaining proper oral hygiene during healing is essential for success.
5. Final Prosthesis Placement
Custom-made, permanent prosthetic is attached once healing is complete
Daily care, including using the 
best toothpaste
, helps extend implant life.
