Why Do My Gums Bleed While Brushing?
Noticing muh se blood aana when you brush teeth can be worrying. Many people ask, “why are my gums bleeding?” or “why do my gums bleed when I brush my teeth?” Gums bleeding is usually a sign that your gums need care. Knowing the causes and how to stop bleeding helps protect your teeth and keeps your mouth healthy.
Why Gums Bleed
Gum bleeding happens for several reasons:
Gingivitis or gum inflammation: Swollen gums bleed easily.
Plaque buildup: Leftover food and plaque irritate gums, causing gum bleeding.
Vitamin deficiency: Low vitamin C or K weakens gums. This answers the question, “what deficiency causes bleeding gums?”
Brushing too hard: Pressing hard or using a stiff toothbrush can cause bleeding gums.
Other factors: Pregnancy, stress, or some medicines can also cause bleeding gums.
How to Stop Bleeding Gums
If your 
gums are bleeding
, try these steps:
Rinse with warm saltwater to clean the mouth and calm gums.
Use a soft toothbrush and brush gently.
Avoid sweets, tobacco, and anything that irritates gums.
Press a clean cloth lightly on the gums to stop bleeding.
These steps give short-term relief. For long-term care, bleeding gums treatment at Dental Kraft is recommended.
How to Prevent Gum Bleeding
You can prevent gums bleeding with simple daily habits:
Brush teeth twice a day and floss every day.
Use mouthwash if your dentist suggests it.
Eat fruits and vegetables rich in vitamin C and K.
Visit Dental Kraft for regular cleaning and checkups.
Healthy habits keep your gums strong and reduce bleeding gums causes.
When to See a Dentist
See a dentist if:
Bleeding happens often or does not stop.
Gums hurt, swell, or smell bad.
Teeth feel loose or gums are very tender.
Dental Kraft can provide proper bleeding gums treatment, including deep cleaning or 
scaling
 to remove plaque and protect your teeth.
Tips for Healthy Gums
Use a soft toothbrush and replace it every 3–4 months.
Brush gently for 2–3 minutes, covering all teeth.
Floss daily to remove food trapped between teeth.
Eat vitamin-rich foods to strengthen gums.
Get regular checkups at Dental Kraft.
FAQs
1. Why are my gums bleeding only when I brush?
It usually happens because gums are sensitive or plaque is present. Gentle brushing helps.
2. Can vitamin deficiency cause gum bleeding?
Yes. Low vitamin C or K can make gums fragile and lead to gum bleeding.
3. How can I stop gum bleeding immediately?
Rinse with warm saltwater, brush gently, and avoid irritants.
4. Is gum bleeding serious?
If it happens often, it may indicate gum disease. Early care prevents it from getting worse.
5. How often should I see a dentist?
Every six months, or as recommended by Dental Kraft, to prevent bleeding gums causes.
