About Dr Ruchi

Rebuilding smiles, one careful plan at a time.

Dr Ruchi Goel is a prosthodontist: a dentist who specialises in replacing and rebuilding teeth. Her work covers dental implants, every type of denture, crowns and bridges, smile design and full-mouth rehabilitation.

She topped the AIIMS MDS entrance examination in 2012 and completed her postgraduate training in prosthodontics at AIIMS New Delhi, after clinical years at PGIMER Chandigarh and PGIMS Rohtak.

Patients mention one thing most often: she explains the plan, the alternatives and the cost before anything begins.

Is this for you?

When an implant makes sense.

{i:tooth-alert}A lost toothYou've lost one or more teeth to decay, gum disease or an accident.
{i:teeth}A loose dentureA removable denture or bridge feels loose or uncomfortable.
{i:shield-check}Shifting teethYou want to stop neighbouring teeth from drifting into the gap.
{i:smile}Eating freelyYou'd like to bite into an apple again without thinking about it.
How an implant works

A new root, then a new tooth.

A small titanium post is placed in the jawbone where the root used to be. Over 3 to 6 months the bone bonds to it.

A connector (abutment) and a custom crown then sit on top, so you get a tooth that stays put, without cutting down the neighbouring teeth as a bridge does.

Your options

One tooth or a full set.

Single implantOne missing tooth, one implant, one crown.
Implant bridgeSeveral missing teeth in a row, held by two or more implants.
All-on-4 / All-on-6A full set of fixed teeth on four to six implants.
Implant-retained dentureYour denture clicks onto two to four implants and stops slipping.
With bone graftingIf bone has shrunk after a long gap, it can often be rebuilt first.

Aftercare

Looking after your implant

  • {i:check}Soft food, and no hard chewing on that side for the first few days.
  • {i:check}Brush and floss around it like a natural tooth.
  • {i:check}Don't smoke. It raises the risk of the implant failing.
  • {i:check}Come in for a check-up every 6 months.
